Connecting monobank

Open Settings → monobank in FinSight and follow the steps; you approve the connection in the mono app. FinSight only reads your accounts and statement; it cannot make payments. FinSight asks monobank for a statement about once a minute, so a first import of several months can take several minutes; FinSight shows how long is left.

Several monobank cards

When you connect, tick every card you want to import. Later, Settings → monobank lists your other cards in the app’s currency with Add, and each card’s page has Disconnect card. With two or more cards, every imported expense shows which card it came from, and the filter on Expenses can show one card.

Questions in Search

Type a question into Search, such as “how much on coffee in October” or “biggest purchase last week”, and FinSight answers it from your expenses. FinSight counts the answer itself; on an iPhone with Apple Intelligence it also understands everyday words it doesn’t know, such as “on the cat”.

Importing from CSV

Settings → Import from CSV reads a statement or another app’s export. Files from monobank, Privat24, Monefy and Spendee are recognised by themselves; for any other file you choose the date and amount columns. Expenses already in FinSight are skipped as duplicates, and Undo This Import on the result screen removes the whole import.

iCloud backup and restore

Settings → iCloud Backup keeps a copy of your data in your own iCloud: once a day when you leave the app after a change, or at once with Back Up Now. The last three copies are kept. Restore from Backup replaces the data on this iPhone with a copy. On a new iPhone, FinSight offers the newest copy on the empty Expenses screen. monobank’s access is never in a copy, so connect it again after a restore.

Notifications

FinSight can tell you when a plan or a category limit is nearly used up or passed, when last month’s report is ready, the day before a subscription is charged, and when monobank needs reconnecting. FinSight asks for permission after you save your first plan; turn each kind on or off in Settings → Notifications.

Face ID lock

Settings → Face ID Lock hides FinSight until you unlock it with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode. It locks when you open the app and when you come back after 30 seconds or more. It needs a passcode set on the iPhone. Widgets stay visible.

Language

Settings → Language switches FinSight between Ukrainian and English at once, without a restart. System follows your iPhone’s languages. Number and date formats keep your iPhone’s region.

Apple Pay Auto-Log

Settings → Apple Pay Auto-Log in FinSight shows how to set up a Shortcuts automation that records every contactless Apple Pay payment, from any bank, without opening the app.

AI reports

The comment in a report is written by Apple Intelligence on your iPhone. It needs an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence with it turned on; the report’s figures work on every iPhone.

Your data

Everything stays on your iPhone. See the privacy policy.
