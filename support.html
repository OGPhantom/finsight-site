Connecting monobank

Open Settings → Monobank in FinSight and follow the steps; you confirm the connection in the mono app. FinSight only reads your accounts and statement; it cannot make payments. Because monobank lets apps ask for a statement about once a minute, a first import of several months can take several minutes; FinSight shows how long is left.

Apple Pay Auto-Log

Settings → Apple Pay Auto-Log in FinSight shows how to set up a Shortcuts automation that records every contactless Apple Pay payment, from any bank, without opening the app.

AI reports

The comment in a report is written by Apple Intelligence on your iPhone. It needs an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence with it turned on; the report's figures work on every iPhone.

Your data

Everything stays on your iPhone. See the privacy policy (українською).
