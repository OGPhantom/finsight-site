Privacy Policy

Effective 15 November 2026

In short: FinSight collects no personal data. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your transactions, categories and reports stay on your iPhone.

Who we are

FinSight is an expense tracker for iPhone made by Nikita Storchai (ФОП Сторчай Нікіта Павлович), an individual developer in Ukraine. Questions about this policy: storschajnikita@gmail.com.

What FinSight stores, and where

Everything below is kept in storage on your iPhone, shared only with FinSight's own widgets. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else.

  • Your transactions: amount, currency, date, merchant, category and notes, whether you added them yourself, through Siri or Shortcuts, or imported them from monobank.
  • Your categories and rules, such as “always put this shop in Groceries”.
  • Your reports and their AI comments.
  • Your settings: currency, theme and accent colour.

Like other app data, this can be included in your iPhone's own backups (iCloud or computer), under your control and Apple's privacy terms.

Reports and Apple Intelligence

Report figures are counted by FinSight on your iPhone. The optional comment is written by Apple's on-device model (Apple Intelligence). If you translate a comment, Apple's Translation framework does it on the device; iOS may first download the language from Apple. Your spending is not sent to any cloud service.

Siri, Shortcuts and Apple Pay auto-log

If you set up a Shortcuts automation for Apple Pay, iOS passes the payment's amount, merchant and card name to FinSight on your iPhone, and FinSight saves them as a transaction. The same happens when you add an expense with Siri or Shortcuts. Nothing leaves your device.

Connecting monobank (optional)

FinSight works without a bank. If you connect monobank, FinSight downloads your account list and statement straight from monobank's API (api.monobank.ua) to your iPhone and keeps them there. FinSight only reads: it cannot make payments or change anything in your account. Depending on the version of the app, connecting works in one of two ways.

Through the mono app. FinSight asks monobank for read-only access to your accounts and statement, and the mono app shows you that request; you approve or decline it there. monobank then gives FinSight an access identifier, which is kept in your iPhone's Keychain on this device only. Monobank requires each request to be signed with FinSight's key, and that key is not stored in the app: a small FinSight signing service creates the signature. For this it receives only the technical details of the request (which kind of request it is, the access identifier, the account identifier and the period of a statement) and a check from Apple (App Attest) that the request comes from the genuine FinSight app. It returns the signature and keeps no logs. All it stores is a technical record for each installation of the app: its App Attest key, the environment and app it was issued for, a signature counter and the number of requests in the current hour (deleted after 180 days without use). It never stores request or account identifiers, statements, IP addresses or anything else about you. Your statements, balances and name never pass through it: the app talks to monobank directly.

Through monobank's own page. FinSight opens the bank's page at api.monobank.ua inside the app. You confirm the sign-in in the mono app, and the page gives you a personal token, which FinSight picks up and keeps in your iPhone's Keychain on this device only. FinSight reads nothing else on that page, and the page's cookies are deleted when you close it. The page itself belongs to monobank and is covered by monobank's privacy policy.

You can disconnect monobank in FinSight's settings at any time, and withdraw the access in the mono app.

What we share

Nothing. FinSight contains no third-party code that collects data, and we never sell, rent or share information about you.

Deleting your data

Any transaction can be edited or deleted. Settings → Reset Data erases all of FinSight's data, the monobank access included. Deleting the app removes its data from your iPhone; an item in the Keychain may stay until the iPhone is erased, so reset or disconnect monobank first if you want it gone.

Children

FinSight is meant for adults managing their own money. It does not collect data from anyone, including children. monobank's API is not available to clients under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. If FinSight ever starts collecting any data, this page will say so before the app does.
