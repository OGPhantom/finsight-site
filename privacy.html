Privacy Policy

Effective 25 November 2026

In short: FinSight collects no personal data. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. Your transactions, categories, plans and reports stay on your iPhone. The optional iCloud backup goes to your own iCloud, which we cannot see.

Who we are

FinSight is an expense tracker for iPhone made by Nikita Storchai (ФОП Сторчай Нікіта Павлович), an individual developer in Ukraine. Questions about this policy: storschajnikita@gmail.com.

What FinSight stores, and where

Everything below is kept in storage on your iPhone, shared only with FinSight’s own widgets. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else.

  • Your transactions: amount, currency, date, merchant, category and notes, whether you added them yourself, through Siri or Shortcuts, or imported them from a CSV file.
  • Your categories and rules, such as “always put this shop in Groceries”.
  • Your monthly plans and category limits.
  • Your reports and their AI comments.
  • Your settings: currency, theme, language, notifications and the Face ID lock.

Like other app data, this can be included in your iPhone’s own backups (iCloud or computer), under your control and Apple’s privacy terms.

iCloud backup (optional)

If you turn on Settings → iCloud Backup, FinSight saves a copy of its data in your own iCloud: the private CloudKit database of your Apple Account, which only you can read. We never see it, and it does not pass through any server of ours. The newest three copies are kept. You can turn the backup off at any time; the copies already made stay in your iCloud until you delete them in iOS’s iCloud storage settings.

Apple Intelligence on your iPhone

Report figures are counted by FinSight on your iPhone. Several features also use Apple’s on-device models (Apple Intelligence), which run on the iPhone itself:

  • the optional comment in a report;
  • a category for a merchant FinSight doesn’t know (the model sees only the merchant’s name and your category names);
  • reading a word in a question you type in Search, such as “how much on the cat in October” (FinSight counts the answer itself).

If you translate a comment, Apple’s Translation framework does it on the device; iOS may first download the language from Apple. Your spending is not sent to any cloud service.

Siri, Shortcuts and Apple Pay auto-log

If you set up a Shortcuts automation for Apple Pay, iOS passes the payment’s amount, merchant and card name to FinSight on your iPhone, and FinSight saves them as a transaction. The same happens when you add an expense with Siri or Shortcuts. Nothing leaves your device.

Exchange rates

To convert a payment made in another currency, FinSight downloads monobank’s public list of exchange rates (api.monobank.ua/bank/currency). It is the same list for everyone: the request carries no account, no identifier and nothing about you or your payments. FinSight does not connect to your bank and cannot read your bank account.

Notifications

FinSight’s notifications (plan, limits, monthly report, upcoming subscriptions) are scheduled on your iPhone from data on your iPhone. They are local notifications: no push service and no server is involved.

Face ID lock

If you turn on the lock, iOS checks your face, fingerprint or passcode. FinSight only learns whether the check succeeded; it never receives your biometric data.

CSV import and export

FinSight reads only the file you pick to import, such as a statement exported from your banking app, and reads it on your iPhone. An export creates a file that you share where you choose. The exported file isn’t encrypted, so anyone you send it to can read it.

Crash and performance reports

FinSight receives iOS’s crash and performance reports (MetricKit) and keeps the newest 20 on your iPhone. They describe the app, not you, and are never sent anywhere by FinSight. In test builds they can be shared by hand from the testing tools. If you turn on Share With App Developers in iOS settings, Apple may also pass crash reports to us; that is Apple’s service, under Apple’s privacy policy.

What we share

Nothing. FinSight contains no analytics, advertising or other third-party code, and we never sell, rent or share information about you.

Deleting your data

Any transaction can be edited or deleted. Settings → Erase All Data erases all of FinSight’s data on the iPhone, pending notifications included; copies in your iCloud stay until you delete them there. Deleting the app removes its data from your iPhone.

Children

FinSight is meant for adults managing their own money. It does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. If FinSight ever starts collecting any data, this page will say so before the app does.
